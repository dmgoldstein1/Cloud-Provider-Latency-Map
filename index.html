Distribution to each destination — the spread of one metric across all checked sources, for every destination.

What you're looking at

  • Each row is one destination. The box spans Q1 to Q3; the line inside it is the median, and the whiskers reach the min and max.
  • The x-axis shows the current metric — latency (ms), jitter (ms), or loss (%) — and the row is sorted by the statistic chosen in the Sort by buttons.
  • Values outside the range set by the map's range controls are excluded from the box.

How to use it

  • Hover a row to highlight that destination as the pair in the map and matrix; click to read the full list of source → destination values.
  • Check or uncheck sources to change which links feed each distribution; the stats and sort order update live.
  • Check or uncheck destinations in the list to show or hide their rows.

Metric vs metric — how each measured network link compares on two metrics.

What the axes mean

  • x and y: the metrics chosen with the two button rows above the chart — latency (round-trip time, ms), jitter (variation between probes, ms), or loss (% of packets lost).

What you're looking at

  • Each dot is one measured source → destination pair. Dots from checked sources have a white ring.
  • Dot color = the value on the y-axis metric.
  • Gridlines mark intervals; hovering a dot draws a dashed crosshair and highlights that pair in the map and matrix.
  • The dashed white fit line is the least-squares trend through all currently shown dots.

How to interpret it

  • Check or uncheck sources to add/remove their outgoing pairs; the axes rescale to the values shown at that moment.
  • A steep fit line means the two metrics grow together; a flat line means they barely correlate.
  • Dots above the line are worse on the y-axis than the x-axis would suggest; dots below it are better.
  • Click a dot to toggle that source, or hover one to read its exact values.
